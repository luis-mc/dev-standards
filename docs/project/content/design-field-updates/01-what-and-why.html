The starter design system in luismc-docs was written on 1 Nov (ST2, ST3) from a reference design system at its v4 draft. Since then two real projects kept working on their own design systems:

  • Project A approved its v2 on 4 Nov (36 changes) and has more changes in its draft.
  • Project B went from v4 to v9 between 1 and 4 Nov (rules U8 to U18, components 39 to 43) and has U19 and U20 in its draft.

Many of these changes are generic. Any product would want them. This page lists them, says how each was checked, and proposes where each goes in the starter. Product-specific parts stay out (DOCSPEC R15.6).

Neither project is named here. The page says "Project A" and "Project B".
