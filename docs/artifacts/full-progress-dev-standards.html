§ 2How it stays current

This artifact is generated by luismc-artifacts from the record in docs/artifacts/; nothing here is typed by hand.

  • Project tasks (prefix DS) are work in dev-standards that belongs to no feature artifact: releases, fixes, CI, spec changes.
  • Feature tasks link to their feature artifact: AR to Feature: Project artifacts standard, MP to Feature: Model and effort profiles. DS2 and DS5 moved into the models feature and kept their IDs.
  • A status change is one command, set-task-status: it updates the task registry, re-renders every artifact that shows the task and commits the record to main. The end-of-turn hook keeps a session from finishing until the changed artifacts are published.
  • A change merged to main is live through the marketplace’s auto-update.
  • A deleted artifact is recreated from docs/artifacts/; see its README.
